Routing & Service Transparency

1. Real Route-Based Travel Times: Travel times and mode calculations (driving, biking, walking) are powered by the Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM) public API. If OSRM is unreachable, exact Haversine fallback calculations are used with estimated speeds.

2. Free & Open Services: OpenStreetMap tile layers and Nominatim Geocoding are used directly—no paid API keys or subscription setups required.

3. Zero Fabrication Policy: If restaurant business hours aren't manually entered, the app explicitly marks status as Hours Unknown rather than making guesses.
